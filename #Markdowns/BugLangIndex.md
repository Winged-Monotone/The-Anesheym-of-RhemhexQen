Bug People Language Index
- Sacchar - Chitin
- Rhem - the dreamplane of which all anesheym believe they walk upon. The Anesheym believe life is a complex dream. This is one of the fundamental basis of their core belief, "Abyssa, The Anima's Wake."
- Ollah - Egg, particularly used to define the edible eggs the anesheym eat.
- Amrita - the anesheym word for their own honey 
- Scouers - another word for the amalgam.
