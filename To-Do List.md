The Anesheym of Rhemhex'Qen

The Brimdire Wastelands are meant to be a nightmarish hell, a challenging place for later-end characters, and a nightmare to sneak through to reach the city of Rhemhex'qen for younger ones. It wasn't quite chaotic enough, however. That is why now, when you enter these forbidden lands, a massive thunderstorm wrought by the intense antmospheric disturbances caused by the intense greenhouse effect these lands emit, is tearing the landscape apart. Tread with care. We also noted that the use of Orange is typically associated with extradimensional entities, which was confusing to some players. So I've decided to modify the entire pallete of the Sulfuric Wastelands from orange and yellows, to yellow and a new color, Crimson. Some also mentioned that region was difficult to traverse and cross which was slightly intentional but may have been overtuned, as such I've added a property to all of the glass machinery that litters the region making them bridges, allowing you to navigate the area more carefully. 

The first phase of the shah's quest is complete. The queen now awards the player, and is now being prepped for the story itself. The amalgam a central antagonist to the mod has also been recolored to make them more striking. 

Five more foods were added that were left out of the last food update that I had forgotten to add. These foods are related to the new biomes and offer new affects and abilities.
