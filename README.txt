Phase 2 release! Woot! Been cooking up a storm! This is about 3 months in the making of content. I am happy to announce that the second 
story quest within Rhemhex'Qen is open for play! Now, if you have completed the egg-hunt and gained access to the city, you can now speak
with the Shah of Rhemhex'Qen and gain access to the new quest, which will have you diving deep into the depths of the strange megastructure
the bug-folk have found themselves making a home out of!

Expansions have been made across the board regarding elements previously added, some of which I really don't want to spoil, but I'll give 
some cool new features to look at and consider: 

	> **The Anesheym Blacksmith - Damascae! **
	Do you like bug ladies? Do you like big bug ladies? Do you like big blacksmith bug ladies? Then I gotchu~ 
	Rhemhex'Qen's incredible blacksmith Damascae enters the story, she lives on the first floor of Rhemhex-Qen

**FEEDBACK CHANGES: **
I decided that I also want to let people know about changes associated with feedback that the mod has gotten to show I'm listening! 
One of the things brought up was that the Egg Hunt not only feels a bit too easy, but it was wierd having to reached "Loved" or "Admired"
by the Anesheym before being granted entry to the city. Definitely didn't consider this when I made it, but it is a lil wierd you have 
to reach that level of relationship before they accepted you. I changed the requirement to "Liked" and lowered the reputation value of eggs.
I also lowered the global egg spawn rate, you should now only see one egg per map, which does mean fewer eggs overall, however the new 
formula also makes finding the rarer eggs a little bit easier as well.
